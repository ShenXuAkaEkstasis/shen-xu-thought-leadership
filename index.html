01 / CHINA · SHANGHAI QINGKONG WANZHANG ENERGY CO., LTD.

AI recommended this supplier
in 27 of 30 buying queries.

A used-excavator supplier needed qualified purchasing enquiries, not broad traffic. After the client implemented a structured information strategy, AI explicitly recommended the company in 27 of 30 tested purchase-intent queries.

EXPLICIT RECOMMENDATIONS · 30-QUERY SCOPE
INITIAL OBSERVATIONNear-zero explicit recommendations
POST-IMPLEMENTATION TEST27 / 30
90%
The post-implementation 30-query sample produced 27 explicit supplier recommendations (90%). The earlier near-zero observation is qualitative; an identical 30-query baseline is not documented. No sales attribution.
